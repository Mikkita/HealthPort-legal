HealthPort Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-24

HealthPort ("the app", "we") imports fitness history from export files into Health Connect on your Android phone. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

In short

  • Your health data stays on your phone. HealthPort has no servers and no user accounts.
  • HealthPort reads only the export files you choose and writes the data to Health Connect.
  • Usage statistics, crash reports and performance data are on by default. They never contain health data. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.
  • We do not sell data and do not show ads.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Bolek&Lolek, ul. Smorodzowska 13, Wrocław, Poland. Contact: healthport.support@gmail.com.

1. Health data from your export files

When you choose an export file (a ZIP archive or a folder), HealthPort reads it on your phone. It looks for:

  • workouts, including heart rate, distance, calories, route and workout name;
  • sleep, including sleep stages;
  • steps;
  • heart rate;
  • weight and body composition (body fat, bone mass, body water, basal metabolic rate).

For a password-protected archive, you enter the password in the app. It is used only to open the archive on your phone.

All processing happens on your phone. We never receive your export files, their contents or your password, and we have no access to your health data.

HealthPort keeps the data it found ("parsed data") in its private storage on your phone. This lets an import continue after an interruption and lets you add another source or buy the full history without reading the files again. Unpacked files are deleted after reading. Parsed data is deleted automatically 15 days after the last import step (reading a file, adding a source, changing rules or writing), even if you don't open the app. It is deleted so that it cannot be recovered. The app shows the date in Settings and when an import is done. To delete it sooner, open Settings › Delete parsed data now. HealthPort's data is excluded from Android backup.

2. Health Connect

HealthPort works through Health Connect. You decide which permissions to give, and you can change them at any time in Health Connect settings. If you refuse some permissions, HealthPort does not write those data types.

Data HealthPort writes to Health Connect — to add your history:

  • exercise sessions and exercise routes;
  • distance and total calories burned;
  • heart rate;
  • sleep;
  • steps;
  • weight, body fat, bone mass, body water mass, basal metabolic rate.

Data HealthPort reads from Health Connect:

  • exercise sessions, sleep, steps, heart rate, weight — to find records that are already in Health Connect and skip them, so you don't get duplicates;
  • distance, total calories burned, body fat, bone mass, body water mass, basal metabolic rate — only records that HealthPort wrote itself, to update or delete them when you start a new import of the same source;
  • data older than 30 days (a separate permission for past data), because your history is usually older than that.

HealthPort reads this data only during an import that you start (an import can continue in the background, with a visible notification). It shows you only counts in the preview. It does not store, display as charts, or send this data anywhere.

When you start a new import, HealthPort replaces its own earlier records from the same sources with the new data. For a source you imported before but not this time, you choose whether to keep or delete its records. HealthPort never deletes records written by other apps.

Data written to Health Connect stays in Health Connect. Other apps can see it only if you give them permission in Health Connect.

HealthPort's use of data from Health Connect follows the Health Connect permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use this data only for the import features you see in the app. We do not transfer it to anyone, use it for advertising, or sell it.

3. Usage statistics, crash reports and performance data

All three are on by default, as described in the Terms of Use. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Usage statistics. Turning them off stops sending from that moment.

Usage statistics (Amplitude). The app sends events about how it is used, for example: app opened (app language, Android version), permissions given (all, some or none), source app chosen, whether an archive could be read (success or error code), preview shown, import started and finished (free or full, success or error), purchase started, completed or failed, and data deleted. These events never contain health data or the amount of it. With each event, Amplitude receives only: a random analytics ID created by the app, the Android version and platform, the app language, the app version, a session ID, the time of the event and a random event ID used to avoid counting the same event twice. The app does not send your name, email, device brand, manufacturer or model. We have turned off collection of your IP address and location (city and country). Amplitude stores this data in its EU data center.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, Google). When the app crashes, it sends a report: the technical error trace, the kind of source app and the import step, device details at the time of the crash (such as model and Android version) and random installation identifiers. Crash reports never contain your records.

Performance data (Firebase Performance Monitoring, Google). The app measures how fast it works: start time, screen rendering, CPU and memory use, and the duration and result of its network requests (to Amplitude and Google Play). Each report includes device details (such as model and Android version) and a random installation identifier. Google also receives your IP address and uses it only to find the country the report comes from. Performance data never contains health data or the amount of it.

Firebase installation ID. Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring use a random installation ID created by the app (Firebase installation ID). While statistics are on, Performance Monitoring also loads its settings from Firebase Remote Config. With this request Google receives the installation ID, country and language code, time zone, Android version and the app's package name.

The app has no advertising ID and does not use Google Analytics.

Purpose: to understand which features work, to fix errors and to keep the app fast. We do not sell this data. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time by turning statistics off in Settings. Retention: usage statistics — 12 months; crash reports and their installation identifier — 90 days, after which Firebase starts removing them from its systems; performance data linked to your IP address — 30 days, other performance data — 60 days; the installation ID used for settings — until we ask Google to delete it, then Google removes it within 180 days.

4. Purchases

Reading files, the preview and writing the last 30 days are available without a purchase. Importing the full history is a one-time purchase in Google Play.

Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card or bank details. The app checks your purchase on your phone, using information from Google Play (such as the order number and purchase status). Google Play gives us order details for the purchase (such as the order number, product, price, country and date). We keep them for as long as Polish tax law requires — generally 5 years from the end of the year in which the related tax became due. Usage statistics also record that a purchase was started, completed or failed (see section 3). Google's processing of your payment is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice: https://payments.google.com/legaldocument?family=0.privacynotice

Legal basis: performing the purchase contract and our legal obligations (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (c)).

5. Reminders

If you ask for a reminder while your export is being prepared, the app sets a notification on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

6. Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you and keep it for 12 months. Please don't send us your export files or health data. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering your request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), or taking steps related to your purchase when your message concerns it (Art. 6(1)(b)).

7. Who receives data

  • Amplitude, Inc. (USA) — usage statistics, as our processor; stored in the EU.
  • Google (Firebase Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Remote Config) — crash reports and performance data, as our processor.
  • Google Play — purchases, under Google's own terms.

No one receives your health data from us, because we never have it.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area:

  • Firebase Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring and Remote Config are global services: Google may process and store crash reports and performance data wherever Google or its agents have facilities, including outside the EEA (for example, in the USA). Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • Amplitude stores usage statistics in the EU, but Amplitude, Inc. is a US company and its staff may access the data from the US when needed. Amplitude states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; its data processing terms also provide for Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Is providing data required? Automated decisions

You don't have to give us any personal data to use HealthPort. You can turn off usage statistics, crash reports and performance data. Order details are needed only if you make a purchase, because Google Play processes it. We do not make automated decisions or create profiles about you.

9. Deleting your data

  • Data HealthPort wrote to Health Connect: in HealthPort, open Settings › Delete everything HealthPort wrote and confirm. This removes only HealthPort's records, not data from other apps. You can also manage and delete data in Health Connect settings.
  • Data on your phone: parsed data is deleted automatically 15 days after the last import step (see section 1). To delete it sooner, open Settings › Delete parsed data now. Uninstalling HealthPort deletes all its private storage.
  • Usage statistics, crash reports and performance data: turn them off in Settings. To ask us to delete usage statistics already sent, email healthport.support@gmail.com with your statistics ID. You find it in Settings under "Statistics ID". We don't know who you are, so without this ID we cannot find your data. Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days, performance data after 30 or 60 days (see section 3). They are linked only to random installation IDs, which we cannot connect to you.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, object to its processing and to data portability. Where we rely on our legitimate interest (usage statistics, crash reports, performance data, support emails), you can object at any time; for statistics, crash reports and performance data, the switch in Settings does this right away. To use your rights, email healthport.support@gmail.com.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, https://uodo.gov.pl. You can also complain in the EU country where you live.

11. Security

Health data is processed only on your phone and stays in the app's private storage and in Health Connect. Usage statistics, crash reports and performance data are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

12. Children

HealthPort is intended for people aged 16 and over.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and show the new version in the app and on the Google Play page.
