1. What the app does

HealthPort reads export files that you download from fitness apps and writes your history to Health Connect on your Android phone. Before writing, it shows a preview of what it found and what is already in Health Connect.

  • Everything happens on your phone. The app has no servers and no user accounts.
  • It is a one-time transfer of history, not an ongoing sync.
  • You need Health Connect and must allow the app to write data to it. On Android 13 and older, Health Connect is a separate app from Google Play.

2. Your files and your data

  • Import only your own data, or data you are allowed to use.
  • You get export files from other companies. We don't control their content or format, and a company can change its format at any time.
  • Every app exports data its own way. Some records may not be recognized, may be skipped or may differ from what the original app shows. Please check the preview before writing.
  • Keep your original export files. HealthPort doesn't change them, but it doesn't keep a copy of them for you either.
  • When you start a new import, HealthPort replaces its own earlier records from the same sources. For a source you imported before but not this time, you choose what happens to its records. HealthPort never deletes records written by other apps.
  • You can delete everything HealthPort wrote in Settings › Delete everything HealthPort wrote. Parsed data is deleted from your phone automatically after 15 days, or right away in Settings › Delete parsed data now.
